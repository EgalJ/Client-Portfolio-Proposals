# Client Profiles

Three hypothetical clients with different goals, time horizons and risk levels. Each one gets a custom portfolio and a one-page proposal.

---

## Client 1: Maya, 28

**Profile:** Software engineer in Boston. $140k salary. $60k invested ($35k in her 401(k), $25k in a taxable brokerage account). Separate emergency fund already set aside.

**Goals:**
1. Build long-term wealth for retirement
2. Buy a home in about 5 years

**Time horizon:** 35+ years for retirement money
**Risk tolerance:** High. Long horizon, stable income, comfortable with market swings.
**Cash needs:** None from this portfolio right now. Home down payment is saved separately.
**Taxes:** 24% federal bracket. Maxing her 401(k).
**Allocation limits:** 80% to 95% stocks

---

## Client 2: David, 55

**Profile:** Owns an HVAC business he plans to sell in about 3 years for an estimated $4M. $1.5M invested across a taxable account and an IRA. Two kids in college.

**Goals:**
1. Grow and protect his current portfolio
2. Prepare for a large cash payout when the business sells
3. Retire around 60

**Time horizon:** 3 years to the sale, 30+ years in retirement
**Risk tolerance:** Moderate. Most of his net worth is tied up in one business, so the portfolio should diversify away from that risk.
**Cash needs:** About $60k a year for college tuition over the next 4 years
**Taxes:** Top federal bracket. Wants tax-efficient investments.
**Allocation limits:** 50% to 65% stocks, at least 15% in municipal bonds

---

## Client 3: Robert (68) and Linda (66)

**Profile:** Retired couple. $2M portfolio. Social Security covers part of their spending.

**Goals:**
1. Pull $80k a year from the portfolio for living expenses
2. Keep up with inflation
3. Never run out of money, and leave something for their grandkids

**Time horizon:** 25 to 30 years
**Risk tolerance:** Low to moderate. 2008 was stressful for them and they don't want to relive it.
**Cash needs:** $80k a year (a 4% withdrawal rate). Keep 1 to 2 years of spending in short-term bonds.
**Taxes:** Moderate bracket
**Allocation limits:** 30% to 45% stocks, include inflation-protected bonds
